# Product Vision

Dubious Critter Farm is a 4–5 player, 30–60 minute session-based semi-cooperative alien farming game. Four astronauts share one deliberately cramped farm, raise bizarre livestock, fulfill colony contracts, trade through a periodic rocket, handle ecological consequences, and pursue private incentives that do not fully align.

## Core promise
Players should regularly create funny conflict without malicious intent. A player may need a quiet species productive while another needs loud Broodhorns; one may need a juvenile for breeding while another is secretly collecting unique juveniles. Everyone still loses if the colony fails.

## Target match arc
- 0–5 min: land, claim space, hatch early stock.
- 5–15: first production, habitats, trade choices.
- 15–30: breeding, morphs, ecology and strain effects.
- 30–40: rare/high-risk combinations, predators, greed.
- 40–45+: final shipment/extraction and scoring.

Meaningful changes should happen roughly every 60–90 seconds. By minute 5 the farm functions; by 15 the strategy is apparent; by 25 it is compromised; by 35 players improvise; around 45 the run resolves.

## Audience and scale
- First-class target: 4 players.
- Comfortable extension: 5.
- Do not architect for 12 unless essentially free.
- PC first, Steam later.

## What persists
Cosmetics, species unlocks/variants, tools, bestiary knowledge, achievements, titles and starting options may persist. Permanent statistical power and persistent farm state should not.

## First proof
Four astronauts in one ugly shared field raise Broodhorns and Hushmallows. Broodhorn noise disrupts Hushmallow sleep through generic environment systems. If players naturally negotiate placement, adapt, get frustrated and laugh, the project has demonstrated its core.
