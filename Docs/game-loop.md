# Game Loop

## Lobby — ~1–2 min
Choose astronaut cosmetics, unlocked species and starting gear. Some loadout information is public, some private. Assign personal objectives. Session may add random eggs/seeds/wild stock.

## Landing / Claiming — 0–5 min
Players land on one cramped farm, hatch starting stock, establish crude fencing and immediately negotiate space.

## Establish — 5–15 min
First harvests, habitat choices, infrastructure purchases and rocket trades. Initial species interactions become readable.

## Escalate — 15–30 min
Breeding, morphs, stronger products, strains, population pressure, ecological chains and external threats increasingly constrain the plan.

## Crisis / Greed — 30–40 min
Rare mutations, dangerous combinations, valuable contracts, escaped livestock, predators and late tools encourage risky optimization.

## Final Harvest / Extraction — 40–45+ min
Last cargo opportunity, colony objective check, extraction rocket, colony result, then personal scoring and persistent cosmetic/knowledge rewards.

## Rocket cadence
A major rocket cycle occurs roughly every five minutes. It collects limited outbound cargo, brings supplies, exposes limited/random shop stock and contracts, and advances the perceived session clock. Essentials should be dependable; advanced/special stock may be scarce/random.

## Success hierarchy
1. Colony succeeds or fails.
2. Only on colony success do personal objectives meaningfully score.
3. Post-match tone may explicitly mock excellent selfish performance during colony failure.
